# The Payouts: five candidate styles (2026-11-16)

OffCamCelebs is renamed **The Payouts** and moves to top-funnel audits of famous MONEY HEADLINES (Timur's brief, 2026-11-16).
Timur asked for five styles, each adapted (not copied) from an Instagram account that over-performs on views per follower,
with three style frames per style. The reference accounts and screenshots came from his browser research
(`payouts_style_refs_screenshots.zip`; figures below are read from the profile-grid screenshots, not verified here).

| # | Style | Adapted from | What we kept | What we changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marker Sheet | @_sportsball (1.1M followers; recent Reels ~335K–2.9M) | one hand-drawn dot-grid sheet filled in live, top-down on a dark desk, ends on the full sheet | mint highlighter = the headline number, khaki marker = documented figures, a red verdict stamp, label-tape captions |
| 2 | Carve Receipt | @musicledgerhq (153K; ~18K–436K) | dark room, fat serif figures, a cream receipt that carves the pot line by line | green-black room, mint neon headline, khaki verdict plaque, a fairness line, a rendered object instead of a face |
| 3 | Stack Cards | @nbashane_ (10K; up to 1.2M) | stacked cards with "label | $figure", one chunky caption word, a running counter | cream studio, rendered objects instead of photos, mint/khaki chips, "cents kept per headline dollar" counter |
| 4 | Block Table | @explainedusingminecraft (29K; ~13K–153K) | the money acted out with blocks and little figures, one caption word at a time, signs carry the figures | our own tabletop diorama (no game look): one block = $10M, ghost blocks for money that never existed as cash |
| 5 | Audit File | @businessmindsai (21.8K; up to 170K) | media window on top, the story as text below with highlighted keywords | exhibits (documents, the sum) in the window; six short numbered lines that light up one at a time instead of a text wall |

Every frame uses the 035 Powerball figures (already verified) as placeholder content: $847M headline, $363.1M cash,
about $190M kept (our estimate), verdict INFLATED. Sources: `src/*.html`, rendered with `src/shot.mjs` (fonts via @fontsource).
