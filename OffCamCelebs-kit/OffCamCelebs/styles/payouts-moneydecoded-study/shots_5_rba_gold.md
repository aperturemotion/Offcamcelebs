## What v5 teaches in one list
1. Every sentence gets an object that DOES the sentence (digs, opens, counts, shuts, pours, flips, strikes, drives away).
2. Every figure counts up to its value in 0.6–1.6 s and lands on the spoken word.
3. Places are joined by the camera flying (5 whips) or by a hard cut (8); never a fade, wipe or dissolve.
4. Two scenes are LIGHT (the grey quarry, the steel drawers); the rest are a dark-grey studio, not black.
5. The bookend repeats the opening and changes one thing (the truck leaves).
