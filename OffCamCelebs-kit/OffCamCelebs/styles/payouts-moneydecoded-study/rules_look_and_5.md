# Rules read off the @moneydecoded.au references (2026-11-18 study)

Evidence: `study_v5.md` (v5 shot by shot, 17 shots) and `look.md` (measurements over all five films). Numbers are at
720×1280 unless they say 1080; multiply pixels by 1.5 for our 1080×1920. Each rule says what they do; "NEVER" rules say
what does not occur once in five films.

## A. The world and the light

1. **The studio is dark GREY, lit everywhere, not black.** Base tone ≈ #201f21 (R ≈ B, G 1–2 lower). Only 18–40% of a
   frame is under 2% luminance; 34–50% of it is dark grey (5–25%).
2. **Every frame is lit top to bottom.** Top eighth 0.04–0.09, top third 0.10–0.17, peak band (44–56% down) 0.20–0.30,
   bottom edge 0.13–0.20. Peak ÷ top third ≈ 2.
3. **There is always a floor and a backdrop.** The floor is a matte-to-satin dark grey under a soft pool (0.21–0.27 near
   the subject, 0.12–0.18 at the frame's foot); the backdrop is a seamless sweep lit to 0.10–0.17. They meet in a soft
   tonal step at 46–65% of the frame height. No drawn horizon line.
4. **Highlights are rationed.** Only 2.5–4.4% of the frame is over 80%; the 95th percentile is 0.40–0.48.
5. **The vignette is gentle.** Centre ÷ corners = 1.7–3.3 (4.2 in one film); corners stay at pixel value 27–35.
6. **One or two scenes per film are LIGHT** (a grey quarry, steel drawers, a pale map, a blueprint): the brightest sampled frame has
   a mean luminance of 0.38–0.78 in four films (0.22 in v4) against the film's 0.12–0.19. They open the film or carry its longest explanation.
7. **Light acts.** A spot comes up on the object as it is named; a highlight sweeps a surface as the camera rises; a ring
   of light spreads on the floor where something lands; a hit flashes red light on the floor and the sign.
8. **Light pools are visible.** A round pool 50–90% of the frame's width under each subject, its edge soft over 150–250 px.
9. **The image is clean:** no film grain (std ≤ 0.5 of 255 in flat darks), no texture overlay, no chromatic tricks.

## B. Objects

10. **Each sentence has one literal object, and the object DOES the sentence:** a bucket digs, a door opens, a drawer
    shuts, a ladle pours, a sign flips, a gavel strikes, a truck drives away. In v5 all 15 picture shots have an action
    on a word.
11. **Bodies are matte black with soft gradients (0.05 → 0.25 across a face) and a thin, nearly white edge:** highlight
    lines 2–7 px wide (3–10 px at 1080) at 0.92–0.95 luminance, on bevelled edges.
12. **Models are simplified but solid:** rounded bevels, real thickness, legs, bolts, wheels; chunky, toy-like
    proportions; nothing paper-thin except paper.
13. **Bright materials exist and are used for contrast:** brushed steel (0.6–0.9), white paper (0.95), light-grey rock.
14. **Gold is khaki-gold in its body (#886d46–#90754a) and pale cream-gold only in highlights and when self-lit
    (#f6e2be–#f8e9c0).** It is the film's main colour (in 48 of 56 sampled frames of v5).
15. **Colour appears only with a meaning and covers 1.6–2.6% of the frame** (8–9% in one coloured scene per film): gold =
    money; red = the bad turn (#ab3f42–#df2b46); flags and road signs in their own colours; self-lit.
16. **Things arrive and leave by acting:** a sign drops in on two legs; a pedestal telescopes; a stack grows course by
    course; a drawer slides. Nothing fades in.

## C. Figures and text in the scene

17. **Every figure is ON an object or IS an object:** a plate on a sign, a cheque, a price tag, a plinth label, or solid
    extruded numerals standing on a column or the floor.
18. **Figures COUNT UP to their value in 0.6–1.6 s and land on the spoken word** (0 → 80 in 0.6 s; $2.4B → $32B in 1.5 s;
    $450 → $5,930 with ×1 → ×13 in 1.6 s). A growing figure has a growing object beside it (the column, the pedestal).
19. **Type:** heavy, WIDE grotesque capitals and figures, stroke 22–25% of cap height; cap height 5–10% of the frame
    height; a main figure spans 28–65% of the frame's width (up to 94% for the hero figure). Secondary line 55–60% of the
    main one.
20. **Text is self-lit to 0.95–0.98 and haloed:** +0.16–0.29 at the glyph edge, half of it within 2–6 px, the last 10%
    at 30–45 px (45–65 px at 1080). White is warm (#f8f2f2); money figures pale yellow (#f6f2bd, #f9f5dd).
21. **Solid numerals show their sides:** depth 30–40% of their height, lit from above.
22. **Plates are black with a 2–3 px light border, on two thin steel legs or a frame;** a changing value rolls like a
    split-flap or flips in a blur.

## D. Camera

23. **The camera never stops:** still for 1–5% of the running time (18% in one film); the longest hold without motion is
    0.2–2.4 s.
24. **A "hold" still drifts 0.5–2 px a frame** (2–8% of the frame's width per second): a slow push, a slide, a few degrees
    of orbit.
25. **Move mix, share of time:** push-in 13–40%, pull-out 8–18%, lateral track or orbit 16–26%, crane or tilt 13–32%, fast
    travel 5–10%.
26. **Places are joined by flying, not cutting:** a whip of 6–15 frames at 15–60 px/frame (peaks 80–140) with streaked
    blur, landing already decelerating into the next move. v5 has 5 whips and 8 hard cuts in 84 s.
27. **A film has only 9–16 cuts** (including two per card). Shots run 1.7–11 s, mean ≈ 5 s; one or two shots per film run
    10–11 s and carry several sentences in one continuous move.
28. **Framing:** the lit picture is centred 44–51% down the frame; the named object is 40–85% of the frame's width in
    close shots; the subject is cropped by the frame's edge in 32–63% of frames; close, low or high three-quarter angles,
    rarely square-on.
29. **A shot starts close and pulls back as the thing grows** (the column, the pedestal, the quarry), or starts wide-ish
    and pushes in as a figure lands.
30. **Depth of field is strong:** the subject is sharp and anything near the lens or far behind is soft (in-focus detail
    5–35× the out-of-focus detail); foreground objects pass the lens as blurs.
31. **Motion blur is long:** streaks ≈ 0.4–0.5 of the per-frame travel. Captions are never blurred.
32. **Frame 0 is mid-action** with the voice and the first caption already on; **the last shot keeps moving** and ends
    0.6–0.9 s after the last word.

## E. Captions

33. One phrase of 1–4 words (2.4–2.5 on average), split at the sentence's own punctuation.
34. Warm white #e9e2e1, fully opaque, regular-to-medium weight, sentence case, no box or shadow.
35. Glyph box 12–13 px tall (1.0% of frame height; ≈ 27–28 px font at 1080 wide); top of text 13.8% down, baseline 14.8%
    (y ≈ 266–284 at 1920).
36. Median hold 0.77 s; a change takes 1–2 frames; it never moves, scales, highlights or reacts; objects pass behind it.
37. No caption on a turn card.

## F. Turn cards

38. 2–3 per film, at about 13–20%, 40–55% and 65–77% of the running time; 1.3–2.9 s each (median 1.7 s).
39. **Flat type on black with a radial glow. Not a 3-D scene:** no floor, no extrusion, no reflection, no camera move.
40. Lead-in: white #faf0ee, regular, 40–53 px glyph box (3.1–4.1% of frame height), one or two centred lines.
41. Key word: bold italic, 98–124 px tall (7.7–9.7%), 38–81% of the frame's width (median 56%), last or second-to-last.
42. Glow: centred on the key word, peak 0.14–0.33, half at 240–260 px, gone by 420–520 px; the key word's hue at about a
    third of its value.
43. Colour by meaning: amber #f6b050 (explain, problem, a comparison), pale gold #f8f2a8 (a question, a twist), red
    #f55048 (the worst part).
44. Words land one at a time on their spoken onsets (every 0.15–0.35 s), each announced for 1–2 frames by a small
    blurred block; the glow rises over ~6 frames with the key word; hold 0.5–0.8 s; hard cut in from the picture and hard
    cut out to a moving shot.

## G. Story shape (v5 and v1, from the transcripts)

45. Hook in the first 4.5 s as a whole place in action, then the turn ("But in 1997…") with the figure by 10 s and the
    counter-figure by 14.5 s.
46. Card (a question or "Let us explain") at 15–16 s; the explanation in time order; a card "worst part" at 55–77%.
47. Each new fact is a new object in the same world; a 10–11 s continuous shot explains the mechanism.
48. The ending REPLAYS the opening shot and changes one thing (the truck drives off; the sign now reads PARKES) on the
    punchline. No verdict card, logo or call to action.
49. 150–158 words a minute; 60–85 s.

## H. What they NEVER do

- Never a pure-black frame outside a card's first two frames; never a black top third.
- Never a mirror floor with a readable upside-down image of the object or its text.
- Never a fade, dissolve, wipe or 2-D slide between scenes; never a zoom done by scaling a picture.
- Never a locked-off camera for more than ~2 s.
- Never a figure floating as a flat overlay; never a 2-D chart.
- Never a small subject centred in an empty frame (the lit picture spans 79–94% of the width).
- Never grain, scan lines, glitch effects, lens flares or chromatic aberration.
- Never a caption that moves, grows or changes colour; never a caption on a card.
- Never an extruded, lit, reflected turn card.
- Never coloured light used decoratively (colour is 1.6–2.6% of the frame except in one scene per film).
- Never neon outlines round objects: edges are white specular lines 2–7 px wide, not glowing tubes.
- Never a logo bug, progress bar, lower third or end card.

## J. What the current 037 cut does differently (from `ours.json`, the m3d sheets and `world.ts` LOOK)

1. **Half the frame is dead black.** Under 2%: 48% (theirs 18–40%); dark greys 5–25%: 19% (theirs 34–50%); median pixel
   0.026 (theirs 0.03–0.10, three films at 0.09–0.10). Cause: background and fog are #000000, hemisphere light 0.15, floor
   #0C0C0E.
2. **The top of the frame is unlit.** Top quarter 0.01–0.05 (theirs 0.07–0.17). There is no backdrop.
3. **Highlights are blown.** Over 80%: 8.7% of the frame (theirs 2.5–4.4%); 95th percentile 0.70 (theirs 0.40–0.48): big
   white paper faces, white floor hotspots under the calendar, stack and scale, thick glowing white outlines.
4. **The vignette is harsh:** centre ÷ corners 8.0 (theirs 1.7–3.3); `vignette: 0.62`.
5. **The darks are blue:** (30.8, 31.1, 34.1) against their neutral (32, 30.6, 31.5); the floor pools read cold blue-white.
6. **The floor is a mirror.** Readable upside-down text under the board, the calendar, the cards and the nameplate; theirs
   is a rough floor with a blurred pool and no image.
7. **Edges are neon outlines.** The tag, plates and desk carry a continuous glowing white line; theirs are 3–10 px
   specular lines on bevels with no glow.
8. **The turn cards are 3-D extruded letters with a floor reflection and a coloured floor.** Theirs are flat type on black
   with a radial glow, a small white lead-in (3.1–4.1% of height) and a bold-italic key word 38–81% of the width.
9. **The picture sits too low:** centred 61% down (theirs 44–51%), with a black upper frame.
10. **There is no depth of field.** Everything is equally sharp; theirs keeps only the subject sharp.
11. **Grain is added** (`grain: 0.028`); theirs has none.
12. **Bloom is weak and tight** (`0.36 / 0.5 / threshold 1.12`), so figures look hard and the only glow is the outline;
    theirs is a tight core plus a 45–65 px veil on self-lit text only.
13. **Captions are 1.45× too big** (40 px against ≈ 27–28 px) and a little high (y 235 against ≈ 266–284).
14. **Mint and khaki are our own colours.** In the references a figure is warm white or pale yellow, gold is khaki-gold, and
    red is the only alarm colour; a saturated mint figure does not occur. (A brand choice of Timur's; flagged because he
    asked for the closest possible match.)
15. **Objects are flat, thin and front-facing props** (cards, plates, sheets of paper, a nameplate): plates on sticks.
    Theirs are chunky machines and containers with volume (excavator, vault, truck, crate, conveyor, pedestal, gavel) that
    DO something; flat things (a cheque, a bond) appear inside a solid setting (on bars, in a drawer).
16. **Figures mostly do not count.** Ours appear or flip; theirs count up in 0.6–1.6 s beside a growing object.
17. **Several beats have no action** (nameplate orbit, the filing held for ~5 s, the scale); in v5 every shot has one.
18. **Black frames inside travels** (f240, f660, f1400 on the sheets): theirs never go black between places; a whip keeps
    the grey world streaking past.
19. **The ending adds a receipt, a verdict stamp and a sign-off shot.** Theirs replays the opening and changes one thing,
    with no card or call to action. (Our verdict and sign-off are in Timur's brief; flagged for the same reason as 14.)
20. **No light scene.** Every place is dark; each reference has one or two scenes that are light grey or steel-bright.
