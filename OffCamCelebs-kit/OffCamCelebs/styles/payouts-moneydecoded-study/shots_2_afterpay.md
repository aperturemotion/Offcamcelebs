## Script craft
- Hook (0–7 s): the headline with its number in the FIRST sentence ("paid $39 billion", said at 1.4 s), plus a superlative ("the biggest takeover Australia had ever seen"). Then the paradox (7.8 s): "And yet it charges you no interest and no fees". Then the question as a turn card (12.5 s): "So, how does it make any money?".
- Body: a concrete example with small numbers the viewer can hold ("a $200 jacket", "$50 now, then $50 every two weeks", "about $8", "only 80 cents", "almost 10 times as much"), each sentence one object. Then why (psychology: "feels cheaper"), then the consequence for shops, then the side income (late fees $87M vs shop fees $822M: a comparison), turn card "But here's the twist.", the rule, who really pays ("even the person behind you in the queue paying cash").
- Ending: one sentence that restates the hook with the answer inside it: "That's how an app that charges you nothing was worth $39 billion." No sign-off, no verdict, no call to action, no logo.
- 16 sentences, 4–21 words (median 12). Second person throughout ("you", "your debit card"). Numbers rounded and spoken plainly; "about", "almost", "only" used to size them.
- Words per shot: 3–21; figures are never stacked: one new number per shot, two at most when they are compared (192/8, 80c/$8, 87M/822M).
- Sound (measured earlier): −14.1 LUFS, music+effects ~5 dB under the voice, an effect on every landing/flip/count.
