SCRIPT
Afterpay sold for twenty-nine billion dollars. That's the number on every headline.
The buyer's own filing says thirteen point eight.
Let me explain.
In August twenty twenty-one, Jack Dorsey's Square agreed to buy Afterpay.
But it didn't pay in cash. It paid in its own shares.
Every Afterpay share would become point three seven five Square shares.
That day, one Square share was worth two hundred and forty-seven dollars. That's where twenty-nine billion came from.
And here's the catch.
The deal took six months to close. By then, Square had renamed itself Block, and its share price had roughly halved.
So when Block booked the deal with the SEC, the shares it handed over were worth thirteen point eight billion.
To be fair, twenty-nine billion was the real value on announcement day. The deal fixed the number of shares, not the dollars, and Afterpay's owners kept those shares.
So twenty-nine billion was the price on day one. Not the price paid.
Our rating: inflated.
Send us who to audit next.

FACTS
- CLAIMED (Square's own words, press release 1 Aug 2021): "implied value of approximately US$29 billion (A$39 billion)"; fixed exchange ratio of 0.375 Square Class A shares per Afterpay share; implied A$126.21 per Afterpay share, based on Square's US$247.26 close on 30 Jul 2021.
- HEADLINES: "Square paid Afterpay A$39 billion (US$29 billion)" (Wikipedia, Afterpay article; same figure across press coverage).
- VERIFIED (Block Form 8-K/A, 2022): completed 31 Jan 2022 (PST) / 1 Feb 2022 (AET); 113,387,895 shares issued plus 229,457 for vested option settlement.
- VERIFIED (Block Form 10-Q, Q1 2022, Note 9): 113,617,352 Class A shares issued; "The aggregate fair value of the shares issued was $13.8 billion", based on Block's closing price on the acquisition date; total consideration in the table $13,836,622 thousand.
- DERIVED (from the 10-Q): $13,894,266K stock fair value (incl. $66.3M expensed post-combination) ÷ 113,617,352 shares = $122.29 per share on 31 Jan 2022, vs $247.26 at announcement: down 50.5% ("roughly halved").
- Square renamed itself Block on 10 Dec 2021.
