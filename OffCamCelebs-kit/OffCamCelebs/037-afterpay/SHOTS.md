# 037 v2: the shot design (2026-11-18)

Designed from the reference study (`../styles/payouts-moneydecoded-study/README.md`, rules R1–R32), not from the brief's
visual notes (Timur: "completely ignore the visual instructions… come up with the visuals yourself… as similar to the
reference videos as possible"). Script: `VO_SCRIPT.txt` v2.

Every shot: one chunky, literal, toy-like object that ACTS on the words (something happens every 1–1.5 s), big in frame
(40–95% of the width, cropping welcome), in the lit dark-grey studio; figures on objects or as solid numerals, ≤ 3 words,
counting up and landing with a ring of light; warm white by default, pale yellow for money, red only for the fallen
number; no mint, no paper to read, no people or photos (a person is a glossy pawn or a nameplate with a microphone); the
camera always moving; places joined by hard cuts.

Accuracy: $29B is the headline as reported and was accurate on announcement day; $13.8 billion is the filing's fair value of
the shares issued; $247 is the 30 Jul 2021 close; $122 is derived from the filing; nothing says anyone lost money or
misled anyone; no current share price; the rating is on the $29 billion figure as the price paid.
