Headlines said an American company bought Afterpay for $29 billion.

What the documents say: Square (now Block) agreed in August 2021 to pay in its own shares, not cash. Every Afterpay share became 0.375 of a Square share. At Square's closing price of $247.26 on 30 July 2021, that came to about US$29 billion (A$39 billion).

The deal took six months to close. By 31 January 2022 the company had renamed itself Block, and its shares had roughly halved: about $122 each (our figure, worked out from the filing). Block's own quarterly filing put the fair value of the shares it issued at $13.8 billion.

To be fair: $29 billion was the real value on announcement day. The deal fixed the number of shares, not the dollars, and Afterpay's shareholders got the shares they were promised.

Our rating: inflated. The $29 billion was the price on day one, not the price paid.
