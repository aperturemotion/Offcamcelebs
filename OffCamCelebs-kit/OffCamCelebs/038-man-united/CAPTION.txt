Headlines said the Glazers bought Manchester United for £790 million.

What the records say: the 2005 takeover was legal, and more than £500m of it was borrowed (a £265m bank loan plus £275m of other securities). Within about a year, most of that debt had been moved onto the club's own books.

In January 2010, United itself sold about £504m of bonds to pay off the bank loans. So the club's own ticket, shirt and TV money went on paying for its takeover. ESPN estimates the cost of financing the Glazers' ownership had passed £1 billion by 2018.

Today's figures: at 30 June 2026, United's borrowings stood at about £689m (refinanced several times since 2005). Net finance costs for 2025/26 were £69.6m, about £190,000 a day.

To be fair: under the Glazers, United won five Premier League titles and the 2008 Champions League.

Our rating: inflated. £790m was the price on the headline. The club has been paying the bill ever since.
