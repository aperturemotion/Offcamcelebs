# 038 shot design (2026-11-20)

The brief's set list, built to the reference study (`../styles/payouts-moneydecoded-study/README.md`) and P16 (REAL LOGOS AND
RECOGNISABLE OBJECTS, never grey boxes). Brand assets in `assets/brand/` (crest, US flag, ESPN; SOURCES.txt). Script v1
corrected (`VO_SCRIPT.txt`). No faces: people are nameplates (and the references' glossy pawn where a figure is needed).
Colour: gold/amber for money, red for debt and "the worst part", green for the fairness beat. Every number sits ON an object
and changes on it as it is said. Something changes every 1–2 s. Captions top (small, as now), turn cards flat with soft glow.
Two sides from the first 10 s: the Glazer side (US flag, "THE GLAZER FAMILY | TAMPA, FLORIDA") and the club side (crest,
Old Trafford). No "·" on screen: "|".
