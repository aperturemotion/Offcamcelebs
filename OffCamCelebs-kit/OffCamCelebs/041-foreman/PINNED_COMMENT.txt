Sources:
1. AARP The Magazine, 2014: asked whether he earned in excess of $200 million on the grill, Foreman: "Much more. There were months I was being paid $8 million per month."
2. Salton, Inc. Form 8-K, filed December 10, 1999 (SEC EDGAR): the purchase of the right to use the name "George Foreman" in perpetuity and worldwide; $113.75 million in cash plus shares valued at $23.75 million; Foreman's own part $100 million in cash and $10 million in shares; its pro forma notes show royalties of $38.1 million for the fiscal year ended June 26, 1999 and $16.6 million for the 13 weeks ended September 25, 1999
3. Salton, Inc. Form 10-K405 for fiscal 2000, filed September 29, 2000 (SEC EDGAR): the deal closed December 9, 1999
4. Salton, Inc. SEC filings (10-K and S-3/A, 1998–1999): net sales up 67.2% in fiscal 1998; the George Foreman, Juiceman and Breadman lines about 62% of sales
5. World heavyweight champion 1973 and 1994 (beat Michael Moorer, November 5, 1994, aged 45)
