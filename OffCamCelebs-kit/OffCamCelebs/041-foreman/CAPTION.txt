George Foreman said his grill made him more than $200 million. "Much more," he said.

What the record says: Salton, the company that sold the grill, paid royalties to Foreman and his partners on every sale. Its own SEC filings show $38.1 million in the year to June 1999, then $16.6 million in the next three months.

On December 9, 1999, Salton bought the George Foreman name outright, in perpetuity and worldwide: $137.5 million in cash and stock. That's $192.2 million on paper, before the grill's early years.

The catch: two partners shared in the deal. Foreman's own cut of the buyout was $110 million.

Verdict: LEGIT ✅ (the $200 million figure)

To be fair: the early royalties were never broken out in the filings, and sales were already booming.
