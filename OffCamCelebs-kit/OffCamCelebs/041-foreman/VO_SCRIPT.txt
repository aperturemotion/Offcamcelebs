OffCamCelebs 041 — "On Paper" (George Foreman / the grill money, "$200 million") — VO SCRIPT (final, 2026-11-26)
Written by the daily run (no script in the app). Every figure checked at source (Salton, Inc.'s own SEC filings first).

------------------------------------------------------------------ PASTE-READY (as sent to ElevenLabs)
George Foreman said his grill made him more than two hundred million dollars. Much more, he said. We checked the filings.

Foreman was a two-time heavyweight champion of the world. He won the title back at forty-five.

In the nineties, a company called Salton sold a grill with his name on it, and paid royalties on every sale.

Salton's own S.E.C. filings show those royalties. Thirty-eight million dollars in one year. Then sixteen point six million in just three months.

In December nineteen ninety-nine, Salton bought his name outright. Forever. One hundred thirty-seven point five million dollars, in cash and stock.

That's one hundred ninety-two million on paper. And it leaves out the grill's early years.

The catch: two partners shared in the deal. Foreman's own cut of the buyout was one hundred ten million.

To be fair, those early royalties were never broken out, and sales were already booming.

So, two hundred million dollars from a grill? Our rating: legit.

Send us who to audit next.

LINE BY LINE (sources)
- "said his grill made him more than two hundred million dollars. Much more, he said": AARP The Magazine interview, 2014
  (asked "Estimates are that you earned in excess of $200 million on the grill. True?" Foreman: "Much more. There were
  months I was being paid $8 million per month."). His own words: CLAIMED.
- "two-time heavyweight champion … won the title back at forty-five": world heavyweight champion 1973 (beat Joe Frazier,
  22 Jan 1973) and 1994 (beat Michael Moorer, 5 Nov 1994, aged 45: the oldest heavyweight champion).
- "a company called Salton sold a grill with his name on it, and paid royalties on every sale": Salton, Inc. 8-K, filed
  10 Dec 1999 (SEC EDGAR, CIK 878280, accession 0000950137-99-004429): the deal ends "Salton's obligation to pay royalties
  to George Foreman and his partners based on the sale of 'George Foreman' products".
- "Thirty-eight million dollars in one year. Then sixteen point six million in just three months": the same 8-K's pro
  forma notes reverse "royalty payments of $38,096 [thousand] on George Foreman" products for the fiscal year ended
  26 June 1999, and $16,644 thousand for the thirteen weeks ended 25 September 1999. (Royalties to Foreman AND his
  partners: the script never calls them his alone.)
- "In December nineteen ninety-nine, Salton bought his name outright. Forever. One hundred thirty-seven point five
  million dollars, in cash and stock": Salton 10-K405 for fiscal 2000 (filed 29 Sep 2000, accession 0000950137-00-004296):
  "On December 9, 1999, Salton acquired from George Foreman and his partners the right to use in perpetuity and
  worldwide the name 'George Foreman' … $113,750,000 in cash, payable in five annual installments of $22,750,000 …
  and 779,191 shares"; the 8-K values the shares at $23,750,000 (total $137.5M).
- "That's one hundred ninety-two million on paper": $137.5M + $38.1M + $16.6M = $192.2M, all from Salton's filings.
- "it leaves out the grill's early years": the grill launched 1994; royalties before fiscal 1999 are not broken out in
  Salton's filings.
- "two partners shared in the deal. Foreman's own cut of the buyout was one hundred ten million": the 8-K: "The
  purchase price payable to George Foreman is $100,000,000 in cash, payable in five annual installments of $20,000,000
  … and shares of Salton common stock valued at $10,000,000"; each of the two partners $6,875,000 cash + $6,875,000 in
  stock. (The partners are private individuals: never named on screen.)
- "sales were already booming": Salton 10-K405 FY1999 / FY1998: net sales rose 67.2% in fiscal 1998, with the George
  Foreman Grills, Juiceman and Breadman lines about 62% of net sales.
- Verdict: LEGIT — the $200 million figure as the grill's money: $192.2M is on paper from 15 months of royalties and the
  buyout alone, before the grill's first years; his own share of the buyout is $110M.

RE-CHECK BEFORE POSTING: nothing here can change (1999–2000 filings); Foreman died 21 March 2025 (past tense throughout).
