In 2018, Canelo Álvarez signed what was called the richest contract in sports history: $365 million with DAZN and Golden Boy.

What the record says: the deal was 11 fights over five years, worth at least $365 million. His own 2020 lawsuit spells out the pay: at least $35 million a fight, $15 million for the first.

He fought three times under it: Rocky Fielding (December 2018), Daniel Jacobs (May 2019) and Sergey Kovalev (November 2019). In 2020, no fights at all. That September he sued DAZN, Golden Boy and Oscar De La Hoya for at least $280 million. By November they had agreed to part ways, and the terms were never made public.

By the lawsuit's own numbers, his three fights came to about $85 million, not $365 million.

Verdict: INFLATED (the $365 million figure as his pay)

To be fair: the big number was the deal's headline, not his claim. And about $85 million for three fights is still a fortune.
