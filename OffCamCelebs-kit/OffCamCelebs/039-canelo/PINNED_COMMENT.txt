Sources:
1. ESPN, October 17, 2018: Álvarez signs a five-year, 11-fight deal with DAZN worth at least $365 million (CNBC and CBS Sports, the same week: called the richest contract in sports history)
2. ESPN and Law360, September 9, 2020: his lawsuit against DAZN, Golden Boy Promotions and Oscar De La Hoya, seeking at least $280 million; per the complaint, a $35 million minimum per fight from a $40 million licence fee, and $15 million for the first fight (Fielding)
3. Fights under the deal: Rocky Fielding (December 15, 2018), Daniel Jacobs (May 4, 2019), Sergey Kovalev (November 2, 2019)
4. ESPN and CBS Sports, November 6, 2020: Álvarez released from the Golden Boy and DAZN contracts; the lawsuit resolved; terms not disclosed
5. "About $85 million" is $15M + $35M + $35M, the lawsuit's own figures, not a verified payout
