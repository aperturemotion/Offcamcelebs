## 3. Build the places (the brief's sets)
- **One PLACE per set of the brief, 6–8 of them.** Grounds alternate cream and dark (`ground: "cream" | "dark"`).
- **Hand-overs** (`Film.tsx` DEFS, `tr`):
  - trucks (`dir: "R"`) and cranes (`down` / `up`), alternating, with no two consecutive moves alike;
  - give a truck `gap` (≥ 140, enforced) wherever a place's things reach past its frame;
  - the frame masks and the 2000-px crane are automatic.
- **Each place file** (`sets/<place>.tsx`, the pattern is any 029 set):
  - **timings:** from the take's words (`word(S, line, /^word/)`) and cues (`cue(S, n)`);
  - **camera:** keys via `checkKeys` (30–50 f, never overlapping, dead still between them);
  - **light:** `Light` pools;
  - **objects:** ONE `View3D` holding the place's whole model (`rect` reaching past the frame's foot, ≥ y 1900; `eye`
    up and to the right, x 900–1100, y 300–420, D 1700–2200).
- **Each model** (`props/<place>.ts`, the pattern is the 029 prop that is closest):
  - built from the kit: paper as `paper()`, solids as `rbox` or extrusions with real holes, wires and links, lathes;
  - standing things go on `makeFloor`, hanging or leaning things in front of `makeBackdrop`;
  - every object tagged `userData.obj`, and every designed join declared in `userData.pierces`;
  - anything resting on paper seated with `paperTop`; stacked sheets set back by thickness + 0.3 px, their curls never
    higher than the sheet's above;
  - a sheet that must change shape as it moves uses `group.userData.reshape(opts)`.
  - **★ (031) `rbox` and `box` put the FRONT face at `position.z`, depth running back.** A body built as
    `rbox(w, h, D)` at `z = D / 2` spans `−D/2 … D/2`. A part meant for its front face goes at `D / 2`, NOT `D`, and feet go
    at `±(D/2 − inset)`. Writing `D` left the 031 printer's LED, button and feet, and the ticker's panel and feet, 48–120 px
    in front of their bodies.
  - **★ (031) Close a lathe with a DISC, not a profile point at r = 0.** A cap run in to the axis is a ring of degenerate
    triangles, and three-mesh-bvh's triangle test reports them crossing anything resting on them: the gavel's block "crossed"
    its head with 1.4 px between them. Use `CircleGeometry` caps on anything something rests on.
  - **★ (031) Hang a sheet FLAT on the wall (pins, tape), never leaning on it.** A lean throws a wedge of shadow on the
    backdrop that reads as a second panel, a tent card.
  - **★ (031) Each camera framing holds one object.** Space a place's objects so a neighbour's print never shows at a frame's
    edge, and keep every printed or solid object's top at ≥ 660 on screen (the caption band is above it).
- **Motion vocabulary:** `ramp(f, a, b, EASE)` with the `tokens.ts` easings only:
  - SETTLE for in-view actions;
  - GLIDE for entrances from beyond the edge;
  - EXIT for leaving the frame;
  - COUNT for counters;
  - TRAVEL for long moves.

## 4. The take (when the VO lands in `assets/audio/`: the whole instruction, root CLAUDE.md)
Run from the project root, in this order (the tools are the Remotion 029 kit's, moved to this layout in
`scratchpad/vo/`):
1. `python scratchpad/vo/vo_trim.py`: the newest take in `assets/audio/` → `assets/audio/vo_trimmed.wav` (native rate).
   Read the opening lines against `VO_SCRIPT.txt` first (step 0 of the root VO pipeline).
2. `python scratchpad/vo/fw.py`, then `node scratchpad/vo/mergetok.mjs`. Then check the onsets with
   `python scratchpad/vo/onsets.py`.
3. Rewrite the PLAN in `scratchpad/vo/captions_vo.mjs` for this film's places, then run it: it writes `src/audit/cues.ts`
   and `src/r45/blocks.ts`.
4. `python scratchpad/vo/level_vo.py` → `assets/audio/vo_leveled.wav`, then `node scratchpad/vo/wordcheck.mjs` (every
   action's anchor against the take's tokens).
5. `node scratchpad/vo/guardtest.mjs` (run it ALONE: it swaps `cues.ts`), and run the guard against the real transcript.
6. **The bed:** the same song as the previous film unless the user names another. Copy it in, cut it, and set
   `MUSIC_FILE`.
7. **The levels:**
   - `node scripts/build.mjs`, then `node scratchpad/vo/mixprobe.mjs out` (the film's own mix rebuilt offline from
     `index.html`: `out/solo_vo.wav`, `solo_bed.wav`, `solo_sfx.wav`);
   - then `python scratchpad/vo/gatedsep.py` and `node scratchpad/vo/bedenv.mjs`;
   - repeat until the residual is near zero (bed 5.5 dB under the speech-gated voice in every window and in the tail);
   - then `python scratchpad/vo/sfxlevels028.py`.
8. **Score the SFX** in the same pass (`score.ts`, family per job), unprompted.

## 5. The checks before every handback (RULE 54, all with canaries)
1. `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` and `node scripts/build.mjs --audit`.
2. `npx hyperframes@0.8.143 check`: 0 errors.
3. **The pass-through and hidden-edge checks over EVERY frame:** four interleaved workers, `AUDIT=1 node
   scratchpad/sweep029.mjs audit <w> <last> 4 <dir>/a<w> 99999` for w = 0–3. The result must be **0 PEN** (any crossing
   beyond the declared `pierces`) and **0 EDGES**.
3b. **★ (032) The SCREEN's rules, which the 3-D audit cannot see** (the user: "full intersection and violation sweep"):
   `node scratchpad/sweep029.mjs viol 0 <last> 2 <dir> 0.5`, then `python scratchpad/viol032.py <dir> scratchpad/plan.json 0.5`.
   The capture saves each frame's OBJECTS alone (captions, tagged `data-cap` in `Caption.tsx`, and the ground hidden) plus
   every visible caption word's box. The judge proves itself on real-page canaries, then reports:
   - CROSS / NEAR: a caption word over object ink, or within 12 px of it. Must be 0.
   - BAND: object ink above y 660 outside hand-overs. Must be 0.
   - CAPZONE: a caption outside x 90–950, y 300–680. Must be 0.
   - REEL-R / REEL-B: anything under the Reel's buttons (x > 940, y 1000–1700) or in its caption strip (y > 1500). These
     are LISTED. Look at each: text there is a violation; a solid's edge, a shadow or a thing moving through is not.
   - ⚠ Remember which way the camera moves things: a LARGER camY sets things HIGHER on screen (screen = 960 + (y − camY)·z).
4. **Each hand-over's layers:**
   - run `node scratchpad/sweep029.mjs layers <a> <b> 1 <dir>/h<i> 0.25` per hand-over;
   - then `python scratchpad/stale029.py <dir> scratchpad/plan.json` (after writing `plan.json` from `.build/plan.mjs`);
   - the result must be 0 pop-in, 0 pop-out and 0 overlap.
5. **The shade audit:** `AUDIT=1 FRAMES=… node scratchpad/sweep029.mjs lab 0 0 1 <dir> 1`, one settled frame per place.
5b. **★ 0 floating pieces** (2026-10-12, 030: "the camera visual has a floating piece"): `AUDIT=1 FRAMES=… node

## 6. When a render IS asked for
- Run `npx hyperframes@0.8.143 render . -o renders/<name>.mp4 --quality delivery --workers 4 --no-browser-gpu
  --protocol-timeout 1800000 --player-ready-timeout 600000 --browser-timeout 600`, with FFmpeg's winget bin on PATH.
  `--no-browser-gpu` keeps the render on the same renderer the checks used. About 1–2 h for a 64-s film on this machine.
- **★ (031) Launch it DETACHED with `HYPERFRAMES_RENDER_DETACHED=1`** (PowerShell: set `$env:HYPERFRAMES_RENDER_DETACHED = "1"`,
  then `Start-Process node scratchpad/render.mjs … -WindowStyle Hidden`). Without it the CLI cancels the render
  (`render_cancelled_parent_exited`) the moment the process that launched it exits. Delete a dead attempt's
  `renders/.<name>.hf-transaction-*` folder first.
- **Verify the MP4 itself:**
  - `node scratchpad/vo/mixprobe.mjs out`: the film's own mix rebuilt offline (it mixes in Node and is exactly the film's
    length; the old ffmpeg-graph version once wrote a 350-GB file);
  - fresh stills every 48 frames, `node scratchpad/sweep029.mjs frames 0 <last> 48 <dir> 1`;
  - `python scratchpad/verifymp4.py renders/<name>.mp4 <dir> out/mix.wav`: the file, the picture against the stills, and
    the sound against the mix (029: within 0.02 dB, lag 0, correlation 0.998);
  - `python scratchpad/vo/gatedsep.py`: the bed under the voice in every window;
  - `python scratchpad/popmp4.py renders/<name>.mp4`: 0 isolated one-frame flashes.
- Serve it over a range-request host plus a tunnel.
