Every headline said Ryan Reynolds' Mint Mobile sold for $1.35 billion.

What the record says: in 2023, T-Mobile agreed to buy Mint for up to $1.35 billion. On closing day, May 1, 2024, it paid $420 million in cash plus $536 million in T-Mobile shares. The rest depended on Mint hitting performance targets, which is usually where these numbers shrink.

Not this time. In June 2025, T-Mobile dropped the targets and set the rest at the maximum, minus what it had already paid. On October 9, 2026, it paid the final $420 million.

Reynolds wasn't the only owner: reports put his stake at about 25%, roughly $300 million.

Verdict: LEGIT ✅ The $1.35 billion price was paid in full.

To be fair: Reynolds never called it his billion. He joked that T-Mobile beat out a last-minute bid from his mom.
