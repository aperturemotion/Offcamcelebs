Every headline said Tiger Woods turned down $800 million to join LIV Golf. We went looking for the offer.

What the record says: in June 2022, LIV's CEO Greg Norman told The Washington Post the offer was "mind-blowingly enormous… high nine digits." No number.

On August 1, 2022, on Fox News, the host listed $700 million, then $800 million. Norman answered: "somewhere in that neighborhood," and said the offer was made before he was CEO.

Then Golf Saudi's CEO, Majed Al Sorour, told The New Yorker: "I never offered him that money, not even close."

Tiger has never confirmed any number. No contract, no document.

Verdict: UNSUPPORTED (the $800 million figure)

To be fair: Tiger never joined LIV. He stayed with the PGA Tour.
